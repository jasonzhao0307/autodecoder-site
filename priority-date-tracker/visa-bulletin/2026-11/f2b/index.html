F2B Final Action Dates advanced for three areas and held for two

The Final Action Date (Table A) decides when a green card can be approved for a given priority date. For F2B (Unmarried Adult Sons and Daughters of Permanent Residents), All other countries, China (mainland-born) and India each moved from Aug 22, 2019 in October 2026 to Aug 22, 2020 in November 2026. That is an advance of 366 days (≈12.0 months) for each of the three.

Mexico did not change and remains at May 15, 2010. The Philippines did not change and remains at Oct 1, 2013.
