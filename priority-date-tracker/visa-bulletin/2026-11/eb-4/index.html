EB-4 Visa Bulletin November 2026: Final Action & Filing Dates by Country

Published Oct 28, 2026 · Data: November 2026 Visa Bulletin (Oct 22, 2026) · Source: official Visa Bulletin, USCIS filing charts

In the November 2026 Visa Bulletin, no EB-4 date changed. That covers both EB-4 Special Immigrants and EB-4 Religious Workers, in Final Action Dates (Table A) and Dates for Filing (Table B).

For both subcategories, Table A stays at Dec 15, 2022 and Table B stays at Jan 1, 2025 for All other countries, China (mainland-born), India, Mexico and Philippines.

Table B is 748 days (≈24.6 months) ahead of Table A in each case. USCIS says employment-based applicants use Table B for Form I-485 this month.

Is Table B (Dates for Filing) open for I-485 this month?

For November 2026, USCIS says employment-based applicants must use the Dates for Filing chart (Table B) to file Form I-485. If your priority date is earlier than the Table B date for your category and country, you may be able to file your adjustment of status application this month (other eligibility requirements still apply). EB-4 Special Immigrants dates are unchanged from October 2026

For EB-4 Special Immigrants, the Final Action Date (Table A) is Dec 15, 2022 in November 2026. It was Dec 15, 2022 in October 2026, so there is no change. The Dates for Filing (Table B) is Jan 1, 2025 in both months, also no change.

These dates are identical for each of the five columns: All other countries, China (mainland-born), India, Mexico and Philippines. Because nothing moved, there is no largest move or country difference to point to this month. Table B is 748 days (≈24.6 months) ahead of Table A in each column.

Which chart USCIS accepts for EB-4 in November 2026

For November 2026, USCIS says employment-based applicants use the Dates for Filing chart (Table B) for Form I-485. Form I-485 is the application to adjust status to permanent resident.

For EB-4 Special Immigrants and EB-4 Religious Workers, the Table B date is Jan 1, 2025 for each of the five columns. If your priority date is earlier than that date, you may be able to file your adjustment of status application this month. Other eligibility requirements still apply.

Questions

Did EB-4 dates change in the November 2026 Visa Bulletin?

No. For both EB-4 Special Immigrants and EB-4 Religious Workers, the Final Action Date stays at Dec 15, 2022 and the Dates for Filing stays at Jan 1, 2025. This is the same for All other countries, China (mainland-born), India, Mexico and Philippines.

Which chart does USCIS accept for EB-4 in November 2026?

USCIS says employment-based applicants use the Dates for Filing chart (Table B) for Form I-485 in November 2026. For EB-4 Special Immigrants and EB-4 Religious Workers, the Table B date is Jan 1, 2025.

My EB-4 priority date is in 2024. May I file Form I-485 this month?

A 2024 priority date is earlier than the Table B date of Jan 1, 2025. That means you may be able to file your adjustment of status application this month. Other eligibility requirements still apply. The date is the same for EB-4 Special Immigrants and EB-4 Religious Workers.

How far ahead is the EB-4 Dates for Filing chart of the Final Action Dates?

Table B (Jan 1, 2025) is 748 days (≈24.6 months) ahead of Table A (Dec 15, 2022). This is the same for EB-4 Special Immigrants and EB-4 Religious Workers, and for each of the five listed countries.
