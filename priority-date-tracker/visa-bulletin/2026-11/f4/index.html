F4 Visa Bulletin November 2026: Final Action & Filing Dates by Country

Published Oct 28, 2026 · Data: November 2026 Visa Bulletin (Oct 22, 2026) · Source: official Visa Bulletin, USCIS filing charts

In the November 2026 Visa Bulletin, F4 (Brothers and Sisters of Adult U.S. Citizens) Final Action Dates advanced for 2 of 5 chargeability areas and held steady for 3.

The largest move was for All other countries, up 366 days (≈12.0 months) to Oct 22, 2012. China (mainland-born) moved by the same amount to the same date.

India, Mexico and the Philippines had no change.

F4 Final Action Dates advanced for All other countries and China in November 2026

The F4 Final Action Date (Table A) for All other countries moved from Oct 22, 2011 in October 2026 to Oct 22, 2012 in November 2026. That is an advance of 366 days (≈12.0 months). China (mainland-born) shows the same change: Oct 22, 2011 to Oct 22, 2012, also 366 days (≈12.0 months).

Three areas did not move. India stayed at Dec 15, 2006, Mexico stayed at Apr 22, 2001, and the Philippines stayed at May 15, 2008.
