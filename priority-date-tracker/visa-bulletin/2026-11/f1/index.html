The F1 Table B date for All other countries, China (mainland-born) and India advanced 366 days (≈12.0 months), from Feb 1, 2020 to Feb 1, 2021. Mexico had no change and stays at Dec 1, 2008. The Philippines had no change and stays at Apr 22, 2015.

Table B is 10 days ahead for most areas, but 537 days ahead for the Philippines

Table B dates are later than Table A dates, so they let more priority dates qualify. For F1 this month, Table B is 10 days (≈0.3 months) ahead of Table A for All other countries, China (mainland-born) and India. For Mexico, Table B is 70 days (≈2.3 months) ahead of Table A.

The Philippines has the widest gap in F1 this month: Table B (Apr 22, 2015) is 537 days (≈17.6 months) ahead of Table A (Nov 1, 2013).
