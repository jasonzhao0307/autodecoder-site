EB-2 Final Action Dates advanced 90 days for three chargeability areas

In the November 2026 Visa Bulletin, EB-2 Final Action Dates (Table A) advanced for 3 of 5 chargeability areas and held steady for 2. All other countries, Mexico and the Philippines each moved up 90 days (≈3.0 months), from Jan 1, 2025 in the October 2026 bulletin to Apr 1, 2025. That was the biggest EB-2 move this month.

China (mainland-born) stayed at Oct 1, 2021, and India stayed at Nov 1, 2013. India's EB-2 Final Action Date is still the furthest back of the five chargeability areas.

The Final Action Date is the cutoff that controls when a green card can actually be approved. Chargeability is the country your case is counted under, which is usually your country of birth.

EB-2 Dates for Filing (Table B) did not change in November 2026

All five EB-2 Dates for Filing were the same as in October 2026. The date is Mar 15, 2026 for All other countries, Mexico and the Philippines, Jan 1, 2023 for China (mainland-born), and Jan 15, 2015 for India.

For November 2026, USCIS says employment-based applicants must use the Dates for Filing chart (Table B) to file Form I-485. If your priority date is earlier than the Table B date for your country, you may be able to file; other eligibility requirements still apply.

The Dates for Filing chart has a later cutoff date that runs ahead of Table A, so it can let you submit Form I-485 sooner when USCIS allows it. This month, Table B is 348 days (≈11.4 months) ahead of Table A for All other countries, Mexico and the Philippines. It is 457 days (≈15.0 months) ahead for China (mainland-born) and 440 days (≈14.5 months) ahead for India.

Did EB-2 move in the November 2026 Visa Bulletin?

Yes, for three chargeability areas. The EB-2 Final Action Dates for All other countries, Mexico and the Philippines advanced 90 days (≈3.0 months) to Apr 1, 2025. China (mainland-born) stayed at Oct 1, 2021 and India at Nov 1, 2013. None of the EB-2 Dates for Filing (Table B) changed.

Which chart do I use to file Form I-485 for EB-2 in November 2026?

USCIS says employment-based applicants must use the Dates for Filing chart (Table B) for November 2026. If your priority date is earlier than the Table B date for your country, you may be able to file; other eligibility requirements still apply. The EB-2 Table B dates are Mar 15, 2026 for All other countries, Mexico and the Philippines, Jan 1, 2023 for China (mainland-born), and Jan 15, 2015 for India.

What is the EB-2 India Final Action Date in the November 2026 Visa Bulletin?

It is Nov 1, 2013, the same as in October 2026. India's EB-2 Dates for Filing (Table B) date is Jan 15, 2015, which is 440 days (≈14.5 months) ahead of Table A. Over the last 12 bulletins, India's Final Action Date moved from Apr 1, 2013 to Nov 1, 2013, a net +214 days (≈7.0 months).

What is the EB-2 China Final Action Date in the November 2026 Visa Bulletin?

It is Oct 1, 2021, the same as in October 2026. China's (mainland-born) EB-2 Dates for Filing date is Jan 1, 2023, which is 457 days (≈15.0 months) ahead of Table A.
