EB-5 Visa Bulletin November 2026: Final Action & Filing Dates by Country

Published Oct 26, 2026 · Data: November 2026 Visa Bulletin (Oct 22, 2026) · Source: official Visa Bulletin, USCIS filing charts

Nothing changed for EB-5 in the November 2026 Visa Bulletin. Every Final Action Date and Dates for Filing entry matches October 2026.

EB-5 Unreserved is Current (C) for All other countries, Mexico and Philippines. China (mainland-born) stays at Dec 1, 2016 and India at Dec 1, 2023 on the Final Action Dates chart.

EB-5 Rural, High Unemployment and Infrastructure set-asides are Current (C) in both charts for all five country columns.

Is Table B (Dates for Filing) open for I-485 this month?

For November 2026, USCIS says employment-based applicants must use the Dates for Filing chart (Table B) to file Form I-485. If your priority date is earlier than the Table B date for your category and country, you may be able to file your adjustment of status application this month (other eligibility requirements still apply).
