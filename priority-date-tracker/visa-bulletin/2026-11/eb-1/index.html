EB-1 Visa Bulletin November 2026: Final Action & Filing Dates by Country

Published Oct 25, 2026 · Data: November 2026 Visa Bulletin (Oct 22, 2026) · Source: official Visa Bulletin, USCIS filing charts

In the November 2026 Visa Bulletin, the EB-1 Final Action Date for China (mainland-born) moved forward 31 days (≈1.0 months), from Jul 1, 2023 to Aug 1, 2023. This was the only change in EB-1 this month.

India held at Feb 1, 2023. EB-1 stays current (C) for All other countries, Mexico and Philippines.

The Dates for Filing chart (Table B) did not change for any EB-1 area. USCIS says employment-based applicants must use Table B to file Form I-485 this month.

Is Table B (Dates for Filing) open for I-485 this month?

For November 2026, USCIS says employment-based applicants must use the Dates for Filing chart (Table B) to file Form I-485. If your priority date is earlier than the Table B date for your category and country, you may be able to file your adjustment of status application this month (other eligibility requirements still apply). Only China moved in EB-1 Final Action Dates for November 2026

In the November 2026 Visa Bulletin, EB-1 Final Action Dates (Table A) moved forward for 1 of 5 chargeability areas and stayed the same for the other 4. The one move was China (mainland-born), which went up 31 days (≈1.0 months), from Jul 1, 2023 in October 2026 to Aug 1, 2023 in November 2026.

The EB-1 India Final Action Date stayed at Feb 1, 2023, the same as in October 2026. The Final Action Date is the date your priority date must be earlier than before a green card can be issued, so this date sets who can get final approval this month.

EB-1 remains current for All other countries, Mexico and Philippines

EB-1 is current (C) for All other countries, Mexico and Philippines in both Table A and Table B for November 2026, the same as in October 2026. "Current" means there is no backlog. Applicants charged to these areas do not have to wait for their priority date to become current.

These three areas have been current in every bulletin for the past 12 months (since November 2025). They did not advance or retrogress in any of those bulletins.

EB-1 Dates for Filing did not change, and USCIS accepts Table B this month

The EB-1 Dates for Filing (Table B) are the same as in October 2026: Jul 1, 2024 for both China (mainland-born) and India, and current (C) for All other countries, Mexico and Philippines.

For November 2026, USCIS says employment-based applicants must use the Dates for Filing chart (Table B) to file Form I-485. If your priority date is earlier than the Table B date for your category and country, you may be able to file your adjustment of status application this month (other eligibility requirements still apply). Final approval still depends on the Final Action Date.

Table B is 335 days (≈11.0 months) ahead of Table A for China (mainland-born) and 516 days (≈17.0 months) ahead of Table A for India. Because of this gap, some applicants may be able to file an I-485 now (other eligibility requirements still apply), even though final approval depends on the Final Action Date.

EB-1 China moved steadily forward over the past year, while India moved back in recent months

China (mainland-born): Over the last 6 bulletins (since May 2026), the Final Action Date moved from Apr 1, 2023 to Aug 1, 2023, a net +122 days (≈4.0 months). It advanced in 3 bulletins, stayed the same in 3 and retrogressed in 0. Over the last 12 bulletins (since November 2025), it moved from Dec 22, 2022 to Aug 1, 2023, a net +222 days (≈7.3 months), advancing in 7, unchanged in 5 and retrogressing in 0.

India: Over the last 6 bulletins (since May 2026), the Final Action Date went from Apr 1, 2023 to Feb 1, 2023, a net −59 days (≈1.9 months). It advanced in 1 bulletin, stayed the same in 3 and retrogressed in 2. Over the last 12 bulletins (since November 2025), it moved from Feb 15, 2022 to Feb 1, 2023, a net +351 days (≈11.5 months), advancing in 5, unchanged in 5 and retrogressing in 2.

Questions

Is EB-1 current in the November 2026 Visa Bulletin?

EB-1 is current (C) for All other countries, Mexico and Philippines in both Table A and Table B. It is not current for China (mainland-born), whose Final Action Date is Aug 1, 2023, or for India, whose Final Action Date is Feb 1, 2023.

What is the EB-1 India Final Action Date for November 2026?

The EB-1 India Final Action Date for November 2026 is Feb 1, 2023, the same as in October 2026. The India Dates for Filing (Table B) date is Jul 1, 2024, which also did not change.

How much did EB-1 China move in November 2026?

The EB-1 China (mainland-born) Final Action Date advanced 31 days (≈1.0 months), from Jul 1, 2023 to Aug 1, 2023. Its Dates for Filing date stayed at Jul 1, 2024.

Which chart should EB-1 applicants use to file Form I-485 in November 2026?

USCIS says employment-based applicants must use the Dates for Filing chart (Table B) for November 2026. If your priority date is earlier than the Table B date for your country, you may be able to file this month. Other eligibility requirements still apply.
