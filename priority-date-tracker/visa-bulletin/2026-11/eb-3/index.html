Questions

Can I file my EB-3 I-485 in November 2026?

Possibly. For November 2026, USCIS says employment-based applicants must use the Dates for Filing chart (Table B). If your priority date is earlier than the Table B date for your category and country, you may be able to file, but other eligibility requirements still apply. The EB-3 Table B dates are Aug 1, 2024 for All other countries and Mexico, Apr 1, 2024 for China (mainland-born), Jan 1, 2024 for the Philippines, and Jan 15, 2015 for India.

How much did EB-3 India move in the November 2026 Visa Bulletin?

The EB-3 India Final Action Date advanced 21 days (≈0.7 months), from Jan 1, 2014 to Jan 22, 2014. The same move applies to EB-3 Other Workers. India's Dates for Filing did not change and stay at Jan 15, 2015 in both categories. That is 358 days (≈11.8 months) ahead of the Final Action Date.

Did EB-3 Philippines move in November 2026?

No. The EB-3 Philippines Final Action Date stays at Aug 15, 2023, and the EB-3 Other Workers Philippines Final Action Date stays at Jan 1, 2022. The Dates for Filing also did not change: Jan 1, 2024 for EB-3 and Jun 1, 2022 for EB-3 Other Workers.

What is the difference between a Final Action Date and a Date for Filing?

A Final Action Date (Table A) shows which priority dates can have a green card approved. A Date for Filing (Table B) is usually later and shows which applicants may submit their paperwork early. Each month USCIS says which chart to use for Form I-485, the adjustment of status application. For November 2026, employment-based applicants must use Table B.
