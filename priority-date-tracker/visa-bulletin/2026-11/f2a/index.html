F2A Final Action Dates advanced by about one month in November 2026

The Final Action Date is the cutoff that decides when a green card can be approved. Your priority date has to be earlier than this date. In the November 2026 Visa Bulletin, F2A (spouses and children of permanent residents) Final Action Dates advanced for 5 of 5 chargeability areas.

For All other countries, China (mainland-born), India and the Philippines, the F2A Final Action Date moved from Sep 22, 2026 to Oct 22, 2026, an advance of 30 days (≈1.0 months). These four areas share the same date.

Mexico had the biggest move. Its F2A Final Action Date went from Mar 22, 2026 to Apr 22, 2026, an advance of 31 days (≈1.0 months). Mexico's Table A date, Apr 22, 2026, is still earlier than the Oct 22, 2026 date shown for the other four areas.

F2A Dates for Filing stayed Current, and USCIS is using that chart in November 2026

F2A Dates for Filing (Table B) are Current (C) for All other countries, China (mainland-born), India, Mexico and the Philippines in November 2026. This is the same as in October 2026. "Current" means there is no priority date cutoff on that chart.

For November 2026, USCIS says family-sponsored applicants use the Dates for Filing chart (Table B) to file Form I-485, the adjustment of status application. Because F2A Table B is Current, F2A applicants in the United States may be able to file their adjustment of status application this month (other eligibility requirements still apply).

Table B is ahead of Table A for every area listed. It is Current while Table A is Oct 22, 2026 for All other countries, China (mainland-born), India and the Philippines, and Apr 22, 2026 for Mexico.

F2A Final Action Dates have advanced a lot over the past 6 and 12 months

For All other countries, China (mainland-born), India and the Philippines, the F2A Final Action Date went from Aug 1, 2024 to Oct 22, 2026 over the last 6 bulletins (since May 2026). That's a net gain of +812 days (≈26.7 months). It advanced in 5 bulletins, stayed the same in 1 and retrogressed in 0. Over the last 12 bulletins (since November 2025), it went from Feb 1, 2024 to Oct 22, 2026, a net gain of +994 days (≈32.7 months). It advanced in 6, stayed the same in 6 and retrogressed in 0.

For Mexico, the F2A Final Action Date went from Aug 1, 2023 to Apr 22, 2026 over the last 6 bulletins, a net gain of +995 days (≈32.7 months). It advanced in 5, stayed the same in 1 and retrogressed in 0. Over the last 12 bulletins, it went from Feb 1, 2023 to Apr 22, 2026, a net gain of +1176 days (≈38.6 months). It advanced in 6, stayed the same in 6 and retrogressed in 0.

These 6- and 12-month figures cover Final Action Dates (Table A) only.

Can I file an I-485 under F2A in November 2026?

You may be able to. USCIS says family-sponsored applicants use the Dates for Filing chart (Table B) in November 2026, and F2A Table B is Current (C) for all five chargeability areas listed. That means F2A applicants may be able to file their adjustment of status application this month. Other eligibility requirements still apply.

What is the F2A Final Action Date for Mexico in the November 2026 Visa Bulletin?

It is Apr 22, 2026, up 31 days (≈1.0 months) from Mar 22, 2026 in the October 2026 bulletin. This was the biggest F2A move this month. The F2A Dates for Filing (Table B) for Mexico stayed Current (C).

What is the F2A Final Action Date for India, China, the Philippines and all other countries in November 2026?

It is Oct 22, 2026 for All other countries, China (mainland-born), India and the Philippines. Each moved up 30 days (≈1.0 months) from Sep 22, 2026. The F2A Dates for Filing (Table B) for all four stayed Current (C).

How much has the F2A Final Action Date moved in the past year?

Over the last 12 bulletins (since November 2025), the date for All other countries, China (mainland-born), India and the Philippines went from Feb 1, 2024 to Oct 22, 2026, a net +994 days (≈32.7 months). Mexico's went from Feb 1, 2023 to Apr 22, 2026, a net +1176 days (≈38.6 months). In both cases, the date advanced in 6 bulletins and stayed the same in 6.
