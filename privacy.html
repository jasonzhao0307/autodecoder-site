Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Autodecoder LLC ("we", "us") handles information in the apps we publish, including Priority Date Tracker (the "Apps").

Information we collect

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or phone number through the Apps.

Information you enter into an App (for example, your priority date, visa category, or country of chargeability) is stored only on your device and is not sent to our servers.

Network access

The Apps may connect to the internet to download publicly available data, such as the U.S. Department of State Visa Bulletin. These requests do not include your personal information.

Third-party services

The Apps are distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information as described in the Google Privacy Policy. We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

Children's privacy

The Apps are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Data deletion

Because your data is stored only on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at autodecoderllc@gmail.com.
