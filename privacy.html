Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Autodecoder LLC ("we", "us", "our") handles information in the mobile applications we publish on the Apple App Store and Google Play, including Priority Date Tracker (together, the "Apps"). An individual App may provide additional details specific to that App.

1. Information we collect

Our Apps work without accounts. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or location.

Priority Date Tracker: if you subscribe to notifications, the App sends the following to our server (hosted on Amazon Web Services): a random identifier generated by the App, your device's push notification token, the visa category, country of chargeability, and priority date you entered, your app language, and the purchase receipt Apple or Google provides for your subscription. We use this information only to verify your subscription and to send you Visa Bulletin notifications. If you do not subscribe, the information you enter stays on your device.

2. Data from public sources

The App downloads the publicly available U.S. Department of State Visa Bulletin. To keep bulletin data up to date for all users, the App may send the bulletin data it downloaded to our server together with the App's random identifier. This does not include any personal information.

3. In-app purchases and subscriptions

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive or store your payment card information. We receive only the purchase receipt or subscription token needed to confirm that your subscription is active. The privacy policy of Apple or Google applies to the payment transaction.

4. Push notifications

If you subscribe and allow notifications, the App may send you alerts, such as when a new Visa Bulletin is published or your priority date becomes current. Notifications are delivered through Apple Push Notification service or Firebase Cloud Messaging using your device's push token. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

5. How we share information

We do not sell your data, share it with third parties for their own purposes, or use third-party advertising. We rely on the following service providers to operate the Apps, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Amazon Web Services, which hosts our server
  • Apple App Store, StoreKit, and Apple Push Notification service (Apple Privacy Policy)
  • Google Play, Google Play Billing, and Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google Privacy Policy)

Information sent to our server is encrypted in transit.

6. Data retention and deletion

Information stored on your device is deleted when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App. Information on our server is kept for as long as it is needed to provide notifications. To have it deleted, email us from the App's Settings → Send Feedback (the email includes your App identifier), or write to support@autodecoder.app, and we will remove it.

7. Children's privacy

Our Apps are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Your rights

You can delete the data on your device at any time, opt out of notifications in your device settings, and ask us to access or delete the information stored on our server by contacting us using the details below.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
