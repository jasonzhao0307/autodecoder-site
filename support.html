Priority Date Tracker FAQ

When is the data updated?

The U.S. Department of State usually publishes a new Visa Bulletin each month. The app shows the latest bulletin when you open it. You can also tap Refresh to check for updates.

I'm not receiving notifications.

Notifications are a Premium feature. Make sure your subscription is active and that notifications are allowed for Priority Date Tracker in your device settings.

How do I restore my subscription on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to subscribe. Your subscription is restored automatically when you open the app.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by the app store:

  • iPhone / iPad: Manage subscriptions on the App Store
  • Android: Manage subscriptions on Google Play

How do I delete my data?

Uninstalling the app deletes the data on your device. If you subscribed to notifications, some information is also stored on our server. To have it deleted, use Settings → Send Feedback in the app, or email support@autodecoder.app. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Is this legal advice?

No. The app displays public Visa Bulletin data for reference only. Please consult an immigration attorney about your specific case.
